Fuel

A tank follows the vehicle's chassis grade (from the game's module table): Sandbike 90 to 150, Buggy 220 to 300, Light Ornithopter 225 to 400, Medium Ornithopter 450 to 850, Sand Crawler 450 to 750, Transport Ornithopter 1350 to 2500.

Create a vehicle

Writes a new vehicle of yours into the save from the game's own vehicle tables (game build 1.5.15.0): the same rows the game keeps for a vehicle, which Duplicate also writes. The tier is the Mk number of the parts, so Mk6 is the tier 6 template. A part can be changed for any part of the same slot, a unique or another grade. The vehicle is put 10 m in front of you, the way your character faces when the save was written (outside your base when you stand in one), and the preview and the result say where; an ornithopter is put 30 m ahead of you and 7 m up, in the air, and the game settles it. The editor cannot see the terrain or the rocks, so face open ground before you quit to the menu. Nothing asks first: it is written at once after a backup, or queued while the game is running. A vehicle made this way has not been loaded in the game yet, so check it on a copy of your save first.

Components

Condition is each module's durability against its current maximum; a module with no stored maximum shows only its value. The tank size is not stored in the save; the Fuel column takes it from the vehicle's chassis in the game's module table.
