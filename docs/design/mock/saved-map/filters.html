Saved map: filters (mock-up)

Mock-up. Every number and place is example data. It reads no save and makes no request. The review marks (the coloured tags and the part codes) are not part of the design; the switch above hides them.

The reference map’s filters have 62 groups and controls: 24 work from the save or the page, 9 partly, 29 have no data. The 32 that cannot be used yet are in the closed fold at the end of the panel, with the reason for each. Details and decisions: filters-inventory.md in this folder.

Today’s 14 chips are replaced by the groups below; the inventory has the table of where each one went. The map picture here is a placeholder: the real one stays the keyboard-operable canvas.
