Decree pool

    About term and decrees

    Apply sets the term’s active decree and its reigning faction together: one row, the current term in landsraad_decree_term, and an empty choice clears that field. The faction drop-down starts on your own faction, and any faction can be chosen.

    In this term is read from the current term: Active is the term’s active decree, Elected its elected decree, In rotation a decree in the save’s decree rotation. Pool flag is the save’s own disabled column, shown as stored; it does not say whether a decree is in effect. A Set flag button changes the flag of its own row only; it does not make the decree active, the Active decree above does.

    About the counts

    The three numbers count the 25 houses by your contribution in the current term: finished = the top tier (5 of 5) reached, in progress = some contribution but below the top tier, not started = nothing recorded. They add up to 25. “Open” is used only for a house no faction has taken, in the houses-taken line below.

    Tiers. Click a tier to give it; click the highest reached tier again to remove it. Rewards are not written (open question, tau #609).

    About tiers

    Click a tier (the boxes in the opened row, or the five small boxes in the row itself) to give your character that tier: your contribution is raised to that tier's threshold, and contribution is cumulative, so click tier 3 and tiers 1 to 3 are reached. Click the highest reached tier again to remove it (the contribution drops to the tier below, or to none for tier 1). A change shows as pending (outlined, "will be reached" or "will be removed") with an Undo until it is written; the Choose box on each row picks houses for the bulk buttons.

    Open question (tau #609): rewards that the game's representative hands out to the inventory depend on the save's delivery rows, which are not understood yet; until that is proven a tier click changes "reached" only.

    About Fill and Complete

    Fill, Complete and Reopen act for the faction chosen above. It starts on your character's own faction. For another faction, Fill and Complete record that faction's win and progress and skip your own contribution row. Fill chosen changes your contribution and tiers only. Complete chosen takes the house for the chosen faction: its progress goes to the goal, and the winner and the completion are set. Fill faction goal on one house does both in one click. Everything here is sample data and the buttons change only what this page shows.
