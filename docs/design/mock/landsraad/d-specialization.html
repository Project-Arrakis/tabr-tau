About this mock-up
  • The icon is a small original glyph drawn for this page, always with the line's name beside it; the colour only helps. The final glyphs go through the design review, and the research page recommends original glyphs, not the game's art.
  • A group header shows the line, the character's level in it and the house count. Grouping is one choice at a time (None, Region, Specialization): the board's present Group by region switch becomes the Region choice.
  • Sorting by Specialization orders the lines as the Specialization tab does (Combat, Crafting, Exploration, Gathering, Sabotage) and keeps the houses of a line A to Z. Sorting by Level orders by the character's level in the house's line, ties by line, then house. House, Ascending is the board's order and the starting view.
  • This page reorders with CSS only, so the on-screen order and the reading order differ; the editor draws the rows in the order shown.
  • House names are the save's (Mikkarol, Spinette), which the board already shows.
