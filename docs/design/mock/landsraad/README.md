# Landsraad tab: mock-ups (refs #599, #440)

Three alternative mock-ups of the Landsraad tab. They are static pages (inline CSS, made-up sample data, no external request, they work offline from a file; mock-up A also has a short inline script for sorting, finding, jumping, the action buttons and the term and decree controls, see below). They are not the editor, they read no save, and nothing here changes the editor's code. Open any of them in a browser from disk; they work at phone width and on a desktop.

The colour tokens are the editor's own names and values (`design/assets/tokens-*.css`). The controls at the top of each page (not part of the design) switch the look (Fremen, Awakening, Sietch), Dark or Dayside, and show or hide the data-source marks.

Filters, sorting, term tabs and the location pop-up are done with CSS only (radio buttons, `:has()` and `:hover`); the pop-up is the quick peek on a mouse hover, and a click, an Enter key or a phone tap on the place opens the full map (see "Location: hover and click").

## Kept decisions (mock-up A)

Decisions taken on this page. A change to the page keeps all of them; the page is checked against this list at 390 and 1280 px before and after every revision.

* Houses are always alphabetical by default (and break ties in every sort). Every column heading sorts: house, location, state, tiers reached, next tier, faction status.
* Each row has a house symbol (crest tile). In the build it is the house's real symbol at 32 px (a 64 px picture, sharp on a high-density screen), with the name beside it and the symbol and name together as the one 44 px button that opens the house card; the picture is decoration (empty alternative text), the name carries the meaning. A house with no picture keeps the two letters in the tile. The picture is looked up in a fixed list by house name, never built from it. On a light look the symbol is drawn dark.
* The bar above the list has: Show (All, What is left, Not started, In progress, Top tier), House (Any, Open, Taken), a By region grouping button, Expand all and Collapse all, and Jump to house with the "N of 25 houses shown" count at the right. There is no Find box.
* Three glance cards and a proportional bar (green Top tier, blue In progress, dashed Not started), with tooltips; the cards and the bar segments filter the list when clicked.
* Reward tiers are clickable in the collapsed meter and in the opened row: cumulative, click the highest reached tier again to remove it. Every click writes at once.
* Per house: Fill faction goal, Complete and Reopen in the opened row; Fill and Complete (Reopen once taken) on the collapsed row.
* A faction drop-down that starts on your own faction and lets any faction be chosen. The bulk bar has Choose column, Choose all houses, Clear the choice, Fill chosen, Complete chosen and Reopen chosen. There is no "also set faction progress" checkbox.
* Fill changes your contribution and tiers; Complete takes the house for the chosen faction; Fill faction goal does both.
* "Term and decrees" panel, closed by default: term, reigning faction, active and elected decree, the Active decree and Reigning faction drop-downs with Apply, the decree pool with a Set flag button per row.
* **Every click writes at once** (operator decision, 2026-11-13, tau #641): tier boxes, Fill, Complete, Reopen, Fill faction goal, the bulk buttons, Apply, Make active and Set flag. There is no staged or pending state, no Undo, no Write button, no queue and no "writes" badge on the controls; only one small line, "Writes at once", under the bar says so. A control is disabled while its write runs, and the page then shows a short result line and a toast with what was written, or the error text unchanged, and reads the row again from the save. A bulk button for more than one house asks first inline ("Complete 7 houses?" with Yes and Cancel); a single house needs no question. The mock-up page itself still draws pending states with Undo, as it was drawn before this decision; the editor does not, and this list is the record.
* **Reopen does not undo a Complete completely.** It clears `completed`, the winner and the completion time; the faction's progress that Complete raised to the goal stays, and a house that was taken by another faction before is left open, not given back. It writes and removes no reward rows. The tooltip and the About text say so; no button promises an undo. Removing a tier lowers the contribution only; the reward rows that giving the tier wrote stay in the save.
* **The plain Special Vendor decree is hidden from the pool list** (operator decision, tau #634): `SpecialVendorActive` has no category and its effect is not verified. Its row stays in the data and is never changed or deleted by the editor; it is shown only inside a small closed disclosure under the pool, "Hidden: Special Vendor, no category (effect unverified)", with the same Make active and Set flag controls, so it is reachable if the game turns out to use it. The four category vendors (Vehicles, Weapons, Armor, Utilities) stay in the list.
* Location: the row shows the area name (the named region of the map, for example "Hagga Basin South", "The O'dham", "Western Shield Wall", "Harko Village", "Arrakeen") as the main text, with the map ("Hagga Basin", "Deep Desert") as a small dim second line only where it is not obvious from the area name; no finer spot (such as "north-east" or a grid cell) is shown in the row. Hover shows a quick peek; a click opens the map dialog with Open in Saved map. The peek and the dialog keep the Region, Place and From you lines (Region is the area with its map, Place may keep the finer spot). Sorting by Location sorts by area name. No "open?" mark on the location.
* House name card (experimental), from the symbol and the name together.
* Wording of states: "Top tier" (reached by your contribution), "In progress" and "Not started" are about your contribution; "Taken", "Open" and "ahead" are about the factions and stay separate. "Finished" is not used for your contribution.
* Each tier shows its reward (amount and the game's item name; internal id only in the tooltip) with its state: Not reached, Waiting to collect, Delivered; the 6,000 tier follows the swatch rule (see Rewards).
* Long explanations live only in closed "About ..." disclosures; the notes on the page are one short line each.
* There is no pending change, so no row tint and no Pending chip in the State cell. The text "board place" does not appear.

## Actions on the board (mock-up A, refs #599, #440, #609)

**Superseded in part by #641 (see Kept decisions):** the sections below describe the mock-up page, which stages changes as pending with an Undo, a bulk Write button and "writes" marks. In the editor every click writes at once and none of those exist; what each action writes (the table below) is unchanged.

Everything on this page is sample data, and **the buttons change only what the page shows**: nothing is saved, and a reload brings the sample back. The page keeps a small made-up state per house, so the tiles, the stacked bar, the counts, the facts and the sort order follow every click. In the real editor these controls would write to the save, which is why each carries a **writes** mark.

* **Reward tiers are buttons.** Click tier N and the character's contribution is raised to that tier's threshold, so tiers 1 to N are reached (cumulative; the tooltip and the legend line say so). Click the highest reached tier again to remove it: the contribution drops to the tier below, or to none for tier 1. A change shows as pending (outlined, "will be reached" or "will be removed") with an Undo until it is written.
* **Per-house buttons in the opened row:** Fill faction goal, Complete, Reopen. They act for the faction chosen in the drop-down above the list. It starts on the character's own faction; for another faction the action records that faction's win and progress and skips the character's own contribution row.
* **Quick buttons on each collapsed row** (after Faction status, before the chevron): Fill, and Complete (Reopen once the house is taken). They use the same faction drop-down and the same pending and Undo pattern, are disabled with a tooltip when nothing would change, are at least 44 px high on a phone (where they wrap under the row) and are not part of column sorting. **Same-kind buttons have the same width:** Fill, Complete and Reopen share one width in every collapsed row (so Fill always sits in one column and Complete or Reopen in the next), Fill faction goal, Complete and Reopen share one width in the opened row, and the five buttons of the bulk bar share one width.
* **Bulk bar above the list**, like the editor's: Choose all houses, Clear the choice, Fill chosen, Complete chosen, Reopen chosen, and a line "Chosen: N houses. Fill chosen changes N, Complete chosen changes N for Atreides, Reopen chosen changes N." that updates as the Choose box on each row is ticked. Undo all reverts every pending change. There is no "also set the faction progress" checkbox: **Fill chosen** changes your contribution and tiers only, **Complete chosen** takes the house for the chosen faction (its progress goes to the goal, and the winner and the completion are set), and **Fill faction goal** on one house does both in one click.
* **The five small tier boxes in each collapsed row are buttons too**, with exactly the behaviour of the boxes in the opened row (the same code and the same states): click tier N to raise the contribution to tier N (cumulative), click the highest reached tier again to remove it, pending cells are outlined (dashed, with the same "will be reached" or "will be removed" in the opened row) and the house shows Undo. The two sets are kept in step on every change. Each box is a real button with an `aria-label` such as "Tier 3, 3,600, reward 50 Precision Range Finder, House Alexin, not reached", a tooltip that starts with the threshold and the reward ("3,600: 50 Precision Range Finder"), a pointer cursor, a hover and a focus ring, and a hit area at least 24 px high (44 px on a phone) that does not add to the row height. A click on a box does not open or close the row.
* **Next tier text** reads "next 3,600 · 50 Precision Range Finder, 1,100 more" (the next tier, its reward, the amount missing) and is two pieces that never break inside: from 1024 px up it stays on one line (checked at 1024, 1280, 1440 and 1920 px), below that and on a phone the "1,100 more" piece moves down as a whole. The text comes from one function in the script, so the opened and collapsed rows and the sort agree.
* **Row widths.** The row grid was rebalanced and checked at 390 (phone), 901, 1024, 1280, 1440 and 1920 px: no location is cut off or ellipsised, the next-tier text stays whole, and there is no horizontal scroll. The Location column holds the area name: from 1200 px it is wide enough for the longest sample area name ("Western Shield Wall South", "Jabal Dhuhr Western Cliffs", "Jabal Eifrit Foothills") on one line; narrower than that the area name wraps at spaces onto a second line (two lines at most, checked at 390, 901, 1024, 1100, 1280, 1440 and 1920 px) and never pushes the State column. The empty space after the house name was given to Location. **In the real build the column width should be computed from the longest area name in the place table** (with the two-line wrap as the fallback), not fixed as it is in this mock. From 901 px up the row is two lines tall (faction status sits under the tiers) instead of squeezing the tier column; the longer next-tier text with its reward needs that room.
* **Location: hover and click.** Hovering the area name (mouse only) shows the quick peek, a small card with the schematic map and the spot, and says so ("Quick peek. Click the place for the full map."). Clicking or tapping the place (a real button, reachable with the keyboard) opens the full map in a dialog: the schematic map of the region with the spot marked, the Region (the area, with its map), Place (the finer spot) and From you lines of the opened row, and a link-style button **Open in Saved map**. In the real editor that button would switch to the Saved map tab with the place selected; in this mock it only says so and nothing navigates. Esc, the Close button and a click outside close the dialog, and focus returns to the place. A phone has no hover, so the tap is the only way there. A click on the place does not open or close the row.
* **House name card (experimental, may be dropped).** A small card on the house name: hover or keyboard focus on a desktop, a tap on a phone (the name is a button; a click or tap pins the card open on any device, and Esc, the card's close button or a click outside closes it). It shows four things and no internal ids, board slot or full tier list: **Next reward** (the item and amount at the next tier, for example "3,600: 50 Precision Range Finder", or "top tier reached"), **Waiting to collect** (rewards pending at the house representative, for example "1 Syndicate Boots, 50 Improved Holtzman Actuator", or "nothing waiting"), **Faction race** (each faction's progress against the goal and who finished it; the faction wording, "Taken" and "Open", is separate from your contribution) and **Place** (region and place name; the map picture stays on the click of the place). Both reward lines are drawn from the same data as the tier boxes (see Rewards): Next reward is the reward of the next tier, Waiting to collect lists the rewards of the reached tiers that are still waiting at the representative. The house symbol tile and the name are one button, so hovering, tapping or focusing either one opens the same card (the location keeps its own separate map, and nothing else in the row has a hover card). The card does not open or close the row, the Choose box and the other buttons are unaffected, and it is checked at 390 and 1280 px.
* **The Choose box has its own narrow column** (a "Choose" heading, a hairline between it and the house symbol badge), centred on the row; on a phone it is 24 px and sits level with the badge.
* **Control bar.** Jump to house (label and drop-down) is the last item of the bar with Show, House, Group and Rows, and the "N of 25 houses shown" count sits at the right edge of the same bar in small dim text; on a phone the bar wraps, with Jump on its own line and the count under it. The "Showing only House ..." note appears under the bar only while Jump is used.
* **Alphabetical order, sortable headings, Jump to house and the collapsed start are unchanged; the Find box was dropped.**
* **Short text, explanations tucked away.** Each note above the board is one line; the longer explanations (tiers, Fill and Complete, the counts, the board, earlier terms) sit in closed "About ..." disclosures with a plain triangle.

What each action would write in the real editor (the same rules as today's Fill, Complete and Reopen, plus the reward rows of tau #609):

| Action | Rows it touches |
|---|---|
| Tier click (give) | the character's row in `landsraad_task_player_contributions`, plus one reward row in `landsraad_house_rewards` for each tier crossed |
| Tier click (remove) | the character's row in `landsraad_task_player_contributions` only (the page lists no reward rows for a removal) |
| Fill | the character's contribution raised to the top tier (never lowered), one reward row in `landsraad_house_rewards` for each tier crossed, the faction's progress in `landsraad_task_faction_contributions` set to the goal, and the house marked taken by that faction in `landsraad_tasks` (`completed`, `winning_faction_id`, completion time); for another faction the character's own row is skipped |
| Complete | `landsraad_tasks` (`completed`, `winning_faction_id`, completion time) and the faction's progress set to the goal |
| Reopen | clears `completed`, the winner and the completion time in `landsraad_tasks`; progress and contribution are left as they are, so it does not restore the progress Complete raised or a previous winner, and it touches no reward row |

## Rewards (mock-up A, refs #609)

Still sample data, and the buttons still change only what the page shows.

* **What is shown.** Each tier box in the opened row shows the reward of that tier under its threshold: the amount and the game's item name ("10,000 Solari", "1 Extravagant Message", "50 Precision Range Finder", "1 Pincushion Gauntlets"). The game's internal id is only in the box's tooltip. In the collapsed row the five small tier boxes carry the reward in their tooltip ("3,600: 50 Precision Range Finder") and the next-tier text names the next reward. The house name card's **Next reward** and **Waiting to collect** lines come from the same data.
* **Three states per reward**, each with a shape and a word, not colour alone: **Not reached**; **Waiting to collect** (reached, and the delivery rows hold a positive amount: the reward is waiting at the house representative; amber box, hollow diamond); **Delivered** (collected, amount 0; filled square, the existing "recorded as delivered" mark). A tier that is about to change shows "will be reached" or "will be removed" instead.
* **The swatch rule for the 6,000 tier.** While the character does not hold the house swatch, the 6,000 tier shows the swatch ("1 House Tseida Placeables Swatch"); once the swatch is held it shows the house's own 6,000 item ("75 Diamodine blade parts"). A small line under the tiers says "Swatch not held yet" or "Swatch held". Two sample houses show the two kinds: **Tseida** (swatch not held) and **Alexin** (swatch held); every other house is drawn as not held.
* **Writes.** A tier click (giving a tier), Fill, and Fill chosen would write **one reward row per tier crossed** (tau #609); the pending text says which ("would add: 1 Syndicate Boots, 50 Improved Holtzman Actuator"), and for the 6,000 tier the swatch rule decides which item. Removing a tier lists no reward rows. Whether the game then hands the items out at the representative is what #609 settles.
* **Sample.** The item names are the game's own; which house gets which item, which tiers are waiting or delivered, and which houses hold the swatch are made up.

## Term and decrees (mock-up A)

A closed panel above the board (its heading line shows the term, the reigning faction and the active decree). Open, it shows the term (number, start and end on the play clock, reigning faction, active decree, elected decree), the **Active decree** and **Reigning faction** drop-downs with **Apply**, and the **Decree pool** table (id, decree, an "In this term" badge, the pool flag and a **Set flag to 0/1** button per row). The faction drop-down starts on the character's own faction and any faction can be chosen. Decrees are shown by the name a player knows with the internal id as a small second line. These are the controls of the editor's present Landsraad tab, drawn again; here they change only what the page shows, and each carries a **writes** mark.

| Control | Rows it would write (as the present tab does) |
|---|---|
| Apply (Active decree and Reigning faction) | `landsraad_decree_term`: `active_decree_id` and `reigning_faction_id` of the current (latest) term row, both from the two drop-downs in one edit; "none" clears the field to NULL. `elected_decree_id` and `end_time` are not touched by this control. |
| Set flag to 0 / 1 | `landsraad_decrees`: the `disabled` column of that one decree row (picked by its id) only. It does not make the decree active; the Active decree does that. |

The decree pool lists all 11 decrees of the save. The names are readable forms of the ids, except "CHOAM Refining Contract", which is the game's own name; they stay readable forms until checked in game (#608).

**Make active for <faction>** (on every pool row) does what the Active decree drop-down and Apply do, for that row's decree: it makes the decree the active decree of the term, for the faction chosen in the Reigning faction drop-down (which starts on your own faction); the label follows the drop-down. A decree belongs to a faction (the reigning faction), not to a house. It carries a **writes** mark (in the mock-up it shows as pending with Undo; in the editor it writes at once, see Kept decisions); the row that is already active for that faction shows "Active" instead of a button, and the Set flag button of each row is unchanged. It writes the same rows as Apply: `landsraad_decree_term`, `active_decree_id` and `reigning_faction_id` of the current term row, and nothing else.

The reigning faction is one field of the term row, so "for which faction" is the Reigning faction drop-down: the decree is set on the term, and the faction chosen is recorded as the one reigning in it.

## Reading the pages

Every page has three kinds of mark, with a tooltip each, and ends with a table "What comes from the save, and what is still a question":

* **save**: a table and column the save holds (named in the table at the bottom).
* **derived**: worked out by the editor from fields the save holds (tiers reached, top tier, counts, who is ahead).
* **open?**: not known to be in the save, or an open question. Nothing marked this way is claimed as a fact.

The sample data is made up, and so are all the numbers on the pages (the counts on the tiles, the tiers, the contributions and the terms): the houses' names are the ones the board shows, but the regions, places, positions, contributions, terms and play-clock numbers are placeholders (for the deep desert the "cell" uses the editor's lettered grid A1 to I9). The five tiers 1,200 to 6,000 and the goal 4,000 are sample values.

## What is a save field and what is not

* From the save (read-only, as answered on #440): house status (`landsraad_tasks.completed`, `winning_faction_id`), the two factions' progress (`landsraad_task_faction_contributions`), the character's contribution (`landsraad_task_player_contributions`), the tier thresholds and the reward of each tier (`landsraad_task_rewards`), recorded deliveries and rewards still waiting (`landsraad_house_rewards`), whether the house swatch is held (`landsraad_player_one_time_house_rewards`), the terms and their play-clock window (`landsraad_decree_term`).
* Derived: tiers reached, top tier (5 of 5 reached by your contribution), the three counts, which faction is ahead.
* **The location of a house is not an open question.** The save has no column for a place, but every house representative is drawn on its map today (17 on the Hagga Basin, the other 8 on the Deep Desert, Harko Village and Arrakeen), so the names and the positions are known. The source is a fixed table of the house representatives' positions per map (map, region, place name, coordinates), one row per house, built from the game's map data in the same way as the editor's existing place tables (`internal/ops/place-names.json`, made by `tools/placenamesgen`; the region names are in `internal/ops/regions.go`). The location pop-up, the full-map dialog (A, C) and the maps (B) read that table, so the location carries no **open?** mark. The area names and places in these mock-ups are sample text (17 houses on the Hagga Basin with areas such as "Hagga Basin South", "The O'dham", "Western Shield Wall" and the long "Jabal Eifrit Foothills", the other 8 on the Deep Desert, Harko Village and Arrakeen); the real table gives the true area and place, and the row shows the area name. The distance from the character is worked out from that table and the character's position.
* **Open: history depth.** Per the plan on #440 an older save held earlier terms' rows and a new character's save held one term; a save may hold only the current term, in which case the page says so. A term's rows say what the contribution was; they do not say which terms paid out (only the time of the last payout per house is kept). Terms show the game's play-clock window, not a date.
* **Open: names.** See the next section.

## Notes for the build

* **House symbol badge: 32 px.** The mock draws a neutral placeholder (the first two letters of the house name in a coloured square, 32 px) where the real symbol of each house would go; the build should show the symbols at 32 px, with the name beside them so the row never depends on the picture alone.
* The list is alphabetical by house name by default, with ties in every other sort broken the same way.

## Names the game shows (refs #608)

The decree pool, the active and elected decree and every other Landsraad label are drawn with the name a player knows ("Repair and Refining Times"), with the internal id (`RepairAndRefiningTimes`) as a small monospace second line. The names in these pages are readable forms of the ids, not the game's own wording: the exact in-game wording is an open question until the catalog data or the game's strings are checked. A name that cannot be confirmed would say so on the page. Mock-up A draws the controls of the term card, the decree pool and the task board; B and C show the term card and pool as display only. #608 stays open: the names are readable forms of the ids, not confirmed wording.
