# Notice: game art in `internal/web/static/maps/`, `internal/web/static/houses/`, `internal/web/static/lcc/art/` (with its `items/` folder) and `docs/art/landsraad/`

The two map pictures (`hagga-basin.png`, `deep-desert.png`) and the marker icons in `icons/` are Dune: Awakening game artwork.
They belong to Funcom. They are **not** covered by this repository's MIT licence and this project does not license them.

They are included only so the Live Map can show where things are on the map, the same use as in the Dune Docker console they were
copied from (see `internal/notices/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`). This project is unofficial and not affiliated with or endorsed by Funcom.

The 25 house symbols in `internal/web/static/houses/` (one small picture per Landsraad house, 64 px) are also Dune: Awakening game artwork that belongs to Funcom, taken from the game's own interface pictures of the houses. The same terms apply: not covered by this repository's MIT licence, not licensed by this project, used only so the Landsraad tab can show which house is which, and removed on request.

The pictures in `docs/art/landsraad/` (the house symbols at 128 px, the mission lane icons, the specialization banner icons, the decree pictures and the two faction crests, Atreides and Harkonnen, as a white crest and a round medallion each) are Dune: Awakening game artwork that belongs to Funcom, taken from the game's own interface pictures. They are reference copies for people designing the Landsraad tab and are not loaded by the editor. The same terms apply: not covered by this repository's MIT licence, not licensed by this project, and removed on request.

The pictures in `internal/web/static/lcc/art/` (the Command Center's art: the 25 house symbols at 128 px, the five mission lane icons and the five specialization banner icons at 256 px, five specialization emblems at 96 px, nine decree pictures at 128 px, and the two faction crests at 256 px with their two round medallions at about 100 px) are optimized copies of the reference pictures in `docs/art/landsraad/` (the five emblems are derived from the extracted banner pictures: the emblem of each path cut out of its banner, without the shadow and the faded side copies, by `scripts/lcc-emblems.mjs`), and are Dune: Awakening game artwork that belongs to Funcom. The same terms apply: not covered by this repository's MIT licence, not licensed by this project, used only so the Landsraad and Specialization views can show which house, path, decree and faction is which, served from this program's own address with no network request, and removed on request. Two exceptions in that folder: `decrees/fallback.svg`, a neutral picture drawn for this project, which is covered by the repository's licence, and the two pictures in `standards/` (the gold-edged point of each contributor cloth with its wheat ornament, two PNG files of 111 pixels in height), which are design artwork supplied by the project owner for the approved Command Center design, not game artwork. The pictures in `internal/web/static/lcc/art/items/` (96 reward item pictures, 64 px, WebP) are Dune: Awakening game artwork that belongs to Funcom, scaled down from the item pictures of the Dune Docker console, which the editor reads from a local checkout (no network request, nothing is posted anywhere). The same terms apply: not covered by this repository's MIT licence, not licensed by this project, used only so the reward mouse-over of the Landsraad board can show which item a tier gives, served from this program's own address, and removed on request. A reward item with no picture in that folder (the 25 House swatches, and any item the console has no picture of) shows its name only.

`docs/design/landsraad-command-center/art-manifest.json` lists every file with its source, size and hash.

If you hold the rights and want any of these files removed, open an issue on this repository and they will be taken out.
